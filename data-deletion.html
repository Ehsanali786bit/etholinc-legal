Data Deletion Request

Last updated: August 2026

When you connect your Instagram or TikTok account to Etholinc, we access limited public profile data (username, follower count, engagement metrics) to verify your social media presence. You can request deletion of this data at any time.

What Data We Store

  • Your social media username and profile URL
  • Follower count and engagement rate at the time of verification
  • Verification status (verified / not verified)

We do not store your social media passwords, private messages, or any data beyond what is listed above.

How to Request Data Deletion

You have two options:

Option 1 — Inside the app (instant)
Go to your Profile → tap the connected social account → tap Disconnect. This immediately removes all associated social data from your Etholinc profile.
Option 2 — Email request
Send an email to etholinc@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and include your registered email address. We will delete your data within 30 days and send you a confirmation.

Full Account Deletion

To delete your entire Etholinc account and all associated data, go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account inside the app, or send a deletion request to etholinc@gmail.com. All personal data will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

Contact

For any questions about data deletion, contact us at: etholinc@gmail.com
