Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Etholinc ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Etholinc mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you register and use Etholinc:

  • Name, email address, and account role (Brand or Influencer)
  • Profile photo and cover image
  • Bio, niche, and follower count
  • Social media profile data from Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube (follower count, engagement rate, account handle) — only when you connect your accounts
  • Campaign content, applications, and communications within the app
  • Device identifiers for push notifications (via OneSignal)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your Etholinc account
  • To connect brands and influencers for marketing campaigns
  • To verify your social media presence and display it on your profile
  • To send push notifications about campaign activity
  • To improve app features and user experience

3. Social Media Integration

When you connect your Instagram or TikTok account, we access only your public profile data (username, follower count, engagement metrics) as permitted by the respective platform APIs. We do not post on your behalf, access private messages, or store your social media passwords. You can disconnect your social accounts from your profile settings at any time.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — authentication and database
  • Cloudinary — image storage
  • OneSignal — push notifications
  • ZegoCloud — voice and video calls
  • Instagram Graph API — social verification
  • TikTok Login Kit — social verification
  • YouTube Data API — social verification

Each service has its own privacy policy governing their data practices.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with the third-party services listed above that are necessary to operate Etholinc, and only to the extent required to provide the service.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data is deleted within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To request data deletion, visit our Data Deletion page or contact us directly.

8. Children's Privacy

Etholinc is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the app or email.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: etholinc@gmail.com
